# Asset sources and licences

The user confirmed that every CQC asset used here (models and sounds) is licensed for this project (DECISIONS.md, 2026-11-27). Everything in this folder is generated by `tools/assets/bake.mjs` from the sources below; nothing is edited by hand.

## Models (`models/`)

| File | Source | Licence / credit | Changes |
|---|---|---|---|
| `glock.glb` | CQC `cqc/models/g17-glb.glb` (Glock 17 Gen5, Sketchfab) | CC BY 4.0, by **8sianDude** (credit required) | Scaled to the factory length (202 mm), gun space, hidden internals removed, simplified, the frame and slide controls split into parts, textures atlased; the spare magazines, the chambered round, the weapon light and the night-sight emissive map are left out |
| `akm.glb` | CQC `cqc/models/akmn-glb.glb` (AKMN) | Cleared by the user (CQC asset) | Gun space, hidden internals removed, simplified, textures atlased |
| `soldier.glb` | Blackout `models/soldier.glb` (`SK_Soldier_RU`, Sketchfab export) | Cleared by the user | Metres, feet at y = 0, unweighted and face joints removed, textures re-encoded; the rifle is a decimated copy of `akm.glb` |

## Sounds (`sounds/`)

Copied unchanged from CQC `cqc/sounds/` (mono MP3, cut by CQC's `tools/cut_user_sfx.py` / `cut_impact_sfx.py`). Original recordings, as listed in CQC `docs/RELEASE_NOTES.md` and `cqc/sounds/src/LICENSES.md`:

- **Shots:** `pistol1` (freesound 90967, department64), `ak1`–`ak5` (indoor-range AK recording, freesound 402791).
- **Glock handling:** `glk*` (freesound 121765 / 121766, kno-sfx). **Dry fire:** `dry*`, `dryReset*` (freesound 486226, ruben_uitenweerde).
- **AK handling:** `akMag*`, `akRack*` (freesound 675008 serutonin-deprivd, 142892 ceremonialchapstick).
- **Brass:** `brassS*`, `brassR*` (freesound 318964).
- **Hand grabs / cloth:** `pickup*` (freesound 762911, ienba), `cloth*` (freesound 369604, evanboyerman).
- **Magazine drop:** `magDrop*` (freesound 174058, pjkasinski3).
- **Impacts:** `impPla*`, `impFle*` (CC0: dslrguide, InMotionAudio, worthahep88, modusmogulus, u1769092 "VisceralBulletImpacts"; see CQC `cqc/sounds/src/LICENSES.md`).
- **Voices / body:** `grunt1`–`grunt6` (freesound 841313, takareads), `grunt7` (freesound 874593, samrad20), `fall` (user upload).
- **Footsteps (derived here):** `step1`, `step3` from Kenney "Impact Sounds" (CC0); `step2` from "Oculus Audio Pack 01" by Oculus VR, LLC, **CC BY 4.0** (credit required). Mono, low-passed, faded by `tools/assets/sounds.mjs`.

The freesound licences were recorded by CQC as "being confirmed"; credit every CC BY author above before a public release.
