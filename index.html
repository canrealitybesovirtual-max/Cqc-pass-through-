How to play (Enter MR)

Wait for Ready under Start, then tap Enter MR (the whole house). A “Get ready” card shows the controls while the game reads your Space Setup rooms; pull the trigger to start.

  • Whole house: walk anywhere you scanned in Space Setup. Enemies come from other rooms. Walk, don’t run: running, the edge of the scanned area and walls close to your head make the watch vibrate and show a warning (nothing appears in front of you). Tracking loss pauses. Enter MR: one spot is the old stationary mode (a ring on the floor).
  • Glock: right hand on your right hip, squeeze the right grip (sitting: on your right thigh). AKM: right hand over your right shoulder, squeeze. To holster, let go at the hip with the muzzle pointing down; the AKM goes back over the shoulder.
  • Put it down anywhere: let go of a gun anywhere else and it falls, lands and stays like a real one — on your bed, a table, the couch or the floor (a still hand lays it down; a moving hand drops or throws it). Squeeze the right grip on it to pick it up again. Dropped magazines lie there too, with their rounds: pick one up with the left grip; let one go at your left hip to put it back in the pouch.
  • Fire: trigger. A (or X with your left hand on the gun): drop the magazine. B (or Y with your left hand on the gun): Glock slide release, AKM fire mode (safe / auto / semi; the right stick click too).
  • Reload: left grip at your left hip takes a magazine; push it up into the gun. AKM: squeeze the seated magazine with the left hand to pull it out. Rack: left grip on the slide (AKM: the charging handle), pull back, let go. (Holding A 1 s with no magazine reloads by itself, for testing.)
  • Two hands: left grip on the AKM handguard.
  • Watch (left wrist): health, rounds, wave and fire mode. Look at it, then touch it with the right controller (or point at it and pull the trigger) for the menu: RESUME, RESTART, DIFFICULTY, MODE (whole house / one spot), GUN FIT (tilt, height, forward per gun; SAVE keeps it), AMMO LINE, EXIT.
  • Pause: the watch menu, or B + Y with empty hands. Exit: watch → EXIT, or hold Y for 2 s.
  • Afterwards the diagnostics are sent automatically (the page says “Sent ✓”, or “Will retry” on the next load). Copy below still works for pasting into the chat.

The Quest boundary cannot be turned off from a web page. In whole-house mode the game asks for whole-house tracking; if the Quest refuses, the page says so and the boundary applies. Gun fit from the address (for testing): ?gripPitch=5 (degrees, + = muzzle up), &gripY=0.01 (metres, + = gun higher), &gripZ=0.01 (metres, + = gun back toward you); ?gloves=0 hides the gloves; ?physics=off brings back the old rule (a gun let go returns to its slot). The values used are saved with each session.

Probe tools

Probe session: an orange test cube with an info panel to its left. Y shows the depth picture, X toggles the explicit clear, A starts a 60 s timing run, B changes the refresh rate. Hold both grips for 1 s to exit. Boundary probe and VR test scene: look around, then exit the same way.

Diagnostics

Each session's diagnostics (the last session, with your house's scan) go automatically to this project's private diagnostics database (insert-only) after the session ends and when you tap Copy.
