How to play (Enter MR)

Wait for Ready under Start, then tap Enter MR. A “Get ready” card shows these controls while the game reads your Space Setup room; pull the trigger to go on.

  • Safe spot: a ring follows you on the floor. Stand where it is green, with room to turn, and pull the trigger. Amber: something is within arm’s reach (hold the trigger 1 s to play there anyway). Red: move where the arrow points. Step out of the ring during the round and the game pauses.
  • Glock: right hand on your right hip, squeeze the right grip.
  • AKM: right hand over your right shoulder, squeeze the right grip. Left grip on the handguard to aim with two hands.
  • Fire: trigger. AKM selector: right stick click (semi → safe → auto → semi). It starts on semi; your left wrist shows the mode.
  • Reload: A drops the magazine. Take a new one from your left hip with the left grip, then push it up into the gun. (A again with the magazine out reloads by itself, for testing.)
  • Rack: left grip on the slide (AKM: the charging handle), pull back, let go. B: slide release.
  • Pause: B + Y together; pull the trigger to resume. Exit: hold Y for 2 s.
  • Left wrist: wave, health, kills, ammo and fire mode.
  • Afterwards tap Copy below and paste it into the chat.

Gun sits wrong in your hand? Add to the page address, then reload: ?gripPitch=5 (degrees, + = muzzle up), &gripY=0.01 (metres, + = gun higher in the hand), &gripZ=0.01 (metres, + = gun back toward you). The values used are saved with each session.

Probe tools

Probe session: an orange test cube with an info panel to its left. Y shows the depth picture, X toggles the explicit clear, A starts a 60 s timing run, B changes the refresh rate. Hold both grips for 1 s to exit. Boundary probe and VR test scene: look around, then exit the same way.
