Phase 1 headset tests

  1. Probe session. You should see your room with an orange cube ahead. Check the panel's lines.
    • Stand in the room you set up in Space Setup. Hold the right trigger and point at walls for a few seconds.
    • Walk carefully into another room. Point at its walls with the trigger the same way.
    • Put your hand in front of the cube: does your hand hide it? Then press X (clear off), then X again (clear on). Does the room ever turn black or smeared?
  2. Enter MR (play session). Stand still. Press A and wait 60 s. Press B to switch the refresh rate. Press A again and wait 60 s.
  3. Boundary probe. Look around for 20 s.
  4. In each session, notice whether the Quest's own boundary (Guardian) appears.
  5. Exit each session by holding both grips for 1 second. Then tap Copy below and paste it into the chat.
